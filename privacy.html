Spell Showdown Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains what Spell Showdown ("the game", "we") collects, why, and how you can delete it. The same text is shown in the app (Settings > Privacy Policy) and on the hosted Privacy Policy page linked from the App Store.

The short version

  • We collect only what the game needs to work: a random player ID, the display name you choose, your build, your match results, and, if you sign in, your Sign in with Apple ID and your purchases.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • You can delete your data in the app at any time (Settings > Account).

What we collect

  • Player ID: a random identifier the app creates on first launch. It is not tied to your device's advertising ID or any other identifier.
  • Guest secret: a random value that proves a guest's player ID belongs to this device. The server stores only a one-way hash of it.
  • Display name: the name you choose. Other players see it in matches and, for signed-in players, on the leaderboard.
  • Build: your chosen class, weapon and armor.
  • Match history and stats: for each online (Versus) match, the date, both players' display names and builds, the result and in-match statistics (such as HP, chains, attack, defense and healing). If you are signed in, your Practice results are also saved to your account, along with your rating, XP, level and unlocked equipment.
  • Sign in with Apple (only if you sign in): the stable user identifier Apple gives this app. We do not ask Apple for your name or email address, and we do not store them. When you delete your account, we ask Apple to revoke the app's access.
  • Purchases (only if you buy something): Apple's transaction details for the item (product, transaction ID, purchase date, and whether it was refunded), linked to your account so the item shows up on your other devices. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.
  • Session tokens: sign-in sessions are stored on the server only as one-way hashes, and on your device in the iOS Keychain.

We do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifier or browsing history.

Why we use it

We use this data only to run the game: to match you with opponents, host and record matches, save your progress and purchases across devices, show leaderboards, and prevent cheating and abuse. The game server may log anonymous counts for game design (for example, how long the Versus queue took, or whether a match used a computer-controlled rival); these log lines do not contain your player ID, name, IP address or any other identifier. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDK.

Computer-controlled rivals

When no other player is available, Versus may pair you with a computer-controlled opponent run by our server. It uses no data about you beyond the match itself.

Reporting names and moderation

  • You can report another player's display name from the leaderboard or a match result.
  • For each reported name we keep the name, how many times and when it was reported, and where (leaderboard or match). If you report a name while playing with a player ID, we also keep your player ID and the time, so each player counts only once.
  • A name reported by several players is hidden (shown as "Player") and cannot be used until we review it. Names we block are kept, without any player ID, so they cannot be used again.
  • We may suspend a player ID from online play for repeated abuse. This is always a manual decision.
  • Report and suspension records linked to a player ID are deleted when that player deletes their account or guest data.

Who else sees or processes your data

  • Other players see your display name, build, and match results in the matches you play together, and on the leaderboard if you are signed in.
  • Cloudflare hosts the game server and database. Like any network provider, it processes your device's IP address to deliver the connection. The game server itself does not store IP addresses.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and In-App Purchases under Apple's own privacy policy.

We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers or advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you play. We delete it when you delete your account or guest data. Match records you played with other players are kept for their history, but your name and ID are removed from them (you appear as "Deleted player").

Deleting your data

  • Signed in: Settings > Account > Delete account. If you use Sign in with Apple, you confirm with Apple once more. We then delete your account, player, name, build, unlocks, rating, progress and history from our server. We end your sessions on every device and ask Apple to revoke the app's Sign in with Apple access. Purchases remain with your Apple ID, and Restore Purchases returns them if you create a new account. We keep Apple's transaction record for the purchase, no longer linked to you, so refunds and restores keep working.
  • Guest: Settings > Account > Delete my data deletes your guest player from our server and from this device.
  • Removing the app deletes the data stored on your device, but not the copy on our server. Use the options above, or contact Support, to delete the server copy.

Children

Spell Showdown is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact Support and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above. For questions or deletion requests, use Settings > Support in the app, or the Spell Showdown Support page: https://pugpugpugs.github.io/spellshowdown-support/support.html
