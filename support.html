Spell Showdown Support

Answers to common questions. If they don't help, contact us below.

Contact Support

Report a problem or ask a question on our public support tracker. You need a free GitHub account to post.

Contact Support / Report an Issue

Posts there are public. Never include passwords, Apple ID details, payment information, or other sensitive personal information.

Connection and Versus

  • Versus needs an internet connection. Practice works offline.
  • If Versus can't connect, check your Wi-Fi or mobile data, then close and reopen the app.
  • When no other player is available, Versus may pair you with a computer-controlled opponent so you don't wait long.
  • If you lose connection during a match, reopen the app and tap Return to match while the match is still running.

Account and Sign in with Apple

  • On iPhone you can sign in with Apple or play as a guest. Your rating, progress, unlocks and purchases belong to your signed-in account, so they follow you to other devices.
  • We don't ask Apple for your name or email address.
  • To use your account on a new device, install the game and sign in with the same Apple ID.
  • You can sign out in Settings > Account.

Purchases and Restore Purchases

  • Purchases are processed by Apple and belong to your account.
  • On a new device, sign in and tap Restore Purchases in the Shop.
  • For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page. We can't issue refunds directly.

Deleting your account or data

  • Signed in: Settings > Account > Delete account. You confirm with Apple once more, then your account and its data are deleted from our server and this device.
  • Guest: Settings > Account > Delete my data deletes your guest data from our server and this device.
  • Removing the app deletes the data on your device but not the copy on our server. Use the options above, or contact Support.
  • Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Reporting an offensive display name

  • On the leaderboard, tap the player's row, then Report.
  • After a Versus match, tap your opponent's portrait on the result screen, then Report.
  • A name reported by several players is hidden until we review it. Offensive names are blocked for good.

Basic troubleshooting

  • Close the app completely and reopen it.
  • Update to the latest version from the App Store.
  • Check that your device has an internet connection (Versus, sign-in and purchases need one).
  • Restart your device.

When you report a problem

These help us find the problem faster:

  • What happened, and what you expected to happen.
  • The steps that lead to it, if you can repeat it.
  • The app version: on iPhone, Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Spell Showdown shows it.
  • Your device model and iOS version, if relevant.
